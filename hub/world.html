What the demo controls do

They only run this preview. The real agents run on their real schedules, not on this clock.

Demo day
Pause or play the simulated day. Everyone freezes where they are.
Speed
How fast the day runs. At 1× the whole day, 5:30 am to 8:30 pm, takes about three minutes. 4× is four times faster.
Time of day
The clock of the simulated day, with the part of the day beside it. The gold bar shows how far through it is. The sun, moon, sky, lamps and window lights follow this clock.
Sky
Follows the demo clock by default. Choose Real time to match your own clock, or lock one look: dawn, midday, afternoon, sunset or night.
Who is awake
Launch week shows only the agents that switch on first: 16 are working and 6 are asleep (Coach, Analyst, Echo, Brief, Launch, Dispatch). Full team wakes all 22.
Show everyone as
Freezes the whole team in one state, like “Needs you”, so you can compare the faces. Choose the first option to go back to the demo day.
Face lab
A grid of every agent in every state.
Team Room
Click the Team Room card at the bottom to open the group chat. Every message an agent sends or hand-delivers shows up there, with a typing sign first.
Money screen
The big screen behind the YOU podium. Ledger runs it. Every number on it is demo data.
